Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile application "Bour El Maqdis", developed and published by Appsyra.

Information Collection and Use

Bour El Maqdis does not collect, store, share, or process any personal data or sensitive user information.

The app is designed solely for booking items from the Bour El Maqdis store. All bookings are handled directly by the store.

Bour El Maqdis does not support online payments. All payments are made in person at the store. No payment information is collected or processed by the app.

Third-Party Services

The app does not use any third-party services that collect or share user data.

Children’s Privacy

Bour El Maqdis does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13.
